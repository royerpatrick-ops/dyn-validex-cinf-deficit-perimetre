## Description

Distinct corrective v0.2 of a conjectural research note on the expected perimeter deficit of randomized integer convex hulls. A parabolic local model proposes a candidate leading coefficient near 0.719, but its identification with the global finite-radius lattice asymptotic remains unproved. The second-order term is exploratory. The supplied finite-domain and reduced-run script checks are consistency checks, not a proof or a rigorous global error bound. Patrick Royer is an independent researcher without institutional affiliation. The work received substantial assistance from ChatGPT/Codex; Claude contributed initial heuristic, tail and cutoff analyses and numerical checks. No independent human mathematical peer review is attested.
